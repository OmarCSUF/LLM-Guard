\textbf{A date inside the substitute's prompt.} Llama~3.2's chat template writes the current date into the system prompt; Qwen2.5's writes none. The substitute arm's scores therefore depend on the day it is run: re-scored on 25 October rather than 28 September 2026, its probabilities differ from the stored arm by up to 0.058. Every re-scoring of the substitute in this supplement pins the template's date to that of the recorded run.

\textbf{Invariants and tests.} The file \texttt{tests/\allowbreak test\_reading\_\allowbreak invariants.py} checks the stored runs for the two defects that produced plausible numbers without an error. A wrong-step fault, a guard read at a generation step that is not its verdict, fails two checks: that the step read carries at least half the mass on the answer words, and that the score agrees with the guard's own generated verdict except at ties within $3\times10^{-5}$ of 0.5. Further checks confirm that every judge decision is the $0.40/0.60$ band applied to its stored score and every Layer-1 routing decision the $0.3/0.7$ band.
